# Inputs for the supplementary dependence checks

landscape_arrays.npz contains derived environmental histories on the original 500 m grid, restricted to Depression and Plateau cells from 10 km inside to 70 km outside the legal boundary. The fields are cells (row-major grid index), x and y (cell-centre coordinates in EPSG:32735), group (1 Depression, 3 Plateau), distance (signed kilometres from the boundary), forest (at least 30% baseline tree cover), events (first tree-loss threshold-crossing year, NaN otherwise), and fire (22 annual Boolean rows for 2001–2022). These are environmental observations and landscape classifications; the archive contains no patrol, security-event, individual-person or historical-source records. NumPy loads the archive without pickle.

grid.json records the 579 by 447 grid, affine transform and coordinate reference system. profile_chronology.csv records the same two landscape chronologies used in the primary analysis; its long form comes from the retained boundary-surface data. The original wide-form chronology remains in ../governance_profiles/. The three machine labels correspond to fragmented, militia-centred and park-centred control.

blocks_*km_shift*.npz stores geographic-block counts, together with block-grid keys, for 5, 10, 20 and 40 km blocks at the original and half-block-shifted origins. Each matrix has 968 columns: 11 boundary centres, two landscape groups, 22 years and two sides. surface_columns.csv in ../../outputs/dependence_sensitivity/ defines their order. fire_n/fire_y are eligible/burned cell counts; tree_n/tree_y are the original all-cell risk set and tree-loss events; forest_n/forest_y restrict that risk set to baseline-forest cells. The accompanying JSON files record block dimensions and support. A single resampling weight applies to the whole geographic block across all landscapes, years and boundary sides.

The raw sources are the retained landscape-group/covariate rasters, Hansen Global Forest Change v1.12 tree cover and loss timing, ESA FireCCI v5.1 for 2001–2020, and the official monthly continental fire products for 2021–2022. The latter were processed with the original terra average-reprojection workflow and the study's day-100-to-300 window and 25% threshold. raster_provenance.json records hashes for the five extraction rasters. The derived histories exactly reproduce all 1,936 original outcome/boundary/landscape/year/side count pairs, including 42,554 main-corridor cells, 508,320 burned cell-years and 519 tree-loss events. Alternative reprojection implementations did not reproduce every fire count and were not used.

prepare_inputs.py rebuilds geographic-block histories directly from the committed environmental array and checks the counts against the retained primary surfaces. Source rasters are not needed for this statistical reproduction. To repeat upstream extraction, provide the checksum-matched files at the relative locations in raster_provenance.json, set UPEMBA_DEPENDENCE_RASTER_ROOT to their parent and run extract_raster_inputs.py with rasterio 1.5.2. rebuild_fire_terra.R documents the 2021–2022 fire reconstruction if those two extraction rasters must first be recreated from the monthly JD tiles. External acquisition requirements remain documented in ../manifests/ and the existing acquisition/preprocessing scripts.

release_manifest.json checksums the complete new code, data, documentation and retained numerical package. Source products and software retain their own terms; the repository-wide licence does not relicense them. See ../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md. The compact derived environmental inputs are included to make the supplementary statistical analysis reproducible even though some original upstream files were lost.
