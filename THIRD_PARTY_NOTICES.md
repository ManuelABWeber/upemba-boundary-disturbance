# Third-party materials

The repository licences cover the contributors' original work only. They do not relicense satellite products, basemaps, geographic data, reproduced historical material, cited documents, fonts or software dependencies.

Product attribution and versions are in the current manuscript and supplement, config/, data/manifests/, and acquisition-script headers. Obtain source products from their providers and follow their terms. A missing redistribution assessment in an older manifest is not permission to redistribute that input. Original satellite rasters and historical source documents are not included in the current package.

Times New Roman is not distributed here. Install it under a valid licence to regenerate Figure 3. R/Python packages, renv bootstrap files and other third-party code retain their existing licences. Existing attribution/copyright notices take precedence over repository-wide defaults.
